## Statement

The system shall provide a **Share** inspector tab, visible only on atoms the caller owns,
that lists the atom's direct access grants (principal name, principal type, level, grant
date), grants access to a principal — a username or workspace key with a chosen principal
type and an EDITOR or VIEWER level — and revokes an existing grant behind an inline
confirmation, refreshing the grant list after every mutation and surfacing backend errors
through the central mapper.

## Rationale

Atom-level sharing is a branding pillar ("Control") fully supported by the backend but
invisible; owners cannot see or manage who has access. Read-side gating (REQ-FR-260069)
already consumes access levels — this closes the write side of the loop. Owner-only
visibility mirrors the schema: every grant operation is owner-only.

## Acceptance Criteria (behavioral)

- The Share tab appears only when the selected atom's effective access level is OWNER;
  non-owner atoms show no Share tab.
- The tab lists current grants with principal name, type, level, and grant date, and states
  the caller's effective level.
- Granting requires a principal (username for USER, workspace key for WORKSPACE), a
  principal type, and a level limited to EDITOR or VIEWER; success refreshes the list.
- Revoking a grant requires an inline second confirmation; success removes it from the list.
- An unknown principal surfaces the mapped principal-unknown message; other failures surface
  through the central mapper without raw-code parsing in the feature.
- Ownership transfer is not offered (recorded as a deferred slice).

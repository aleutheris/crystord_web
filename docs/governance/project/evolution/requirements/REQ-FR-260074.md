## Statement

The system shall consolidate the header's account controls into a single avatar menu —
identity header, theme control, entries opening the account-settings, workspace-management,
and preferences surfaces, and Sign Out pinned at the bottom — shall provide workspace
management (list and create workspaces; rename, manage members and roles, dissolve) without
any workspace-switching affordance, and shall provide a preferences surface that writes the
shell preferences contract (theme mode, home emphasis, compute badges).

## Rationale

Account, workspace, and preference management need one discoverable home; the top-right was
three loose buttons. The Q3 emphasis switches gained a consumer in EPIC-260069 and need their
toggle UI. Workspace switching is deliberately absent — the backend has no current-workspace
concept (workspaces are sharing principals plus membership), so management is the honest
scope.

## Acceptance Criteria (behavioral)

- The header shows one avatar menu trigger; opening it lists identity (username · email),
  Theme, Account settings, Workspaces, Preferences, and Sign Out pinned last after a
  separator; the menu is keyboard-operable (arrows, Home/End, Escape) and closes on
  outside click.
- Account settings opens the existing account panel unchanged.
- Preferences exposes theme mode, home emphasis, and compute badges; changes persist via the
  shell preferences contract and take effect per their consumers (e.g. emphasis changes the
  landing view on next entry).
- Workspaces lists the caller's workspaces with member counts, supports creating one
  (key, name, description), renaming/description edits, adding a member by username with a
  role, changing a member's role, removing a member, and dissolving a workspace behind a
  two-step confirm; backend errors are surfaced.
- No workspace-switching affordance exists anywhere in the surface.
- Sign Out ends the session exactly as the previous header button did.

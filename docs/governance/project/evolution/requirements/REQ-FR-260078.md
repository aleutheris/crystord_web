## Statement

The system shall provide a **Board** center view in which the user picks one category
dimension and sees its root values as columns (plus an Unassigned column), the working-set
atoms as cards placed by their assignment in that dimension with descendant assignments
rolled up to their root, and recategorizes an atom by moving its card — via drag or an
equivalent accessible move menu — replacing only that dimension's assignment while
preserving all others, through the shared atom-update mutation.

## Rationale

Reclassifying many atoms through the inspector is one-at-a-time slow. A kanban board makes
bulk classification a single gesture per atom, completing the center-view set (Flow ·
Network · Table · Board) on the same working set, selection model, and mutation path.

## Acceptance Criteria (behavioral)

- With no dimension chosen the board prompts for one; the picker lists the accessible
  dimensions.
- Columns are the chosen dimension's root values plus Unassigned; an atom appears under the
  root covering its assignment (exact or descendant), under Unassigned when the dimension is
  unassigned, and under each matching column when multiply assigned.
- Moving a card to a value column assigns that root value; moving to Unassigned clears the
  dimension's assignments; assignments in other dimensions are unchanged; the move persists
  via the shared update mutation and reflects across views.
- Every movable card offers an accessible "Move to…" control equivalent to dragging;
  viewer-level atoms are not movable and offer no move control.
- Card click selects the atom in the shared selection model (inspector opens; selected card
  is visually marked with a non-color-only cue).

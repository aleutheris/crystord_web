## Statement

The system shall provide a **Table** center view — registered through the shell's view
registry — that presents the working-set atoms as rows with columns for title, labels,
content, and a computed indicator; supports inline editing of editable fields through the
shared atom-update mutation; and shares the single selection and working-set model with the
Flow and Network views.

## Rationale

Removing `SearchResultPanel` (EPIC-260066) left no flat/tabular representation of the working
set. The product is pitched to spreadsheet-minded users ("one spreadsheet row = one Atom"), so
a familiar grid is both the regression-closer and the most familiar on-ramp — while the
node-graph remains home (Q1).

## Acceptance Criteria (behavioral)

- Switching to the Table view lists the current working-set atoms as rows (title, labels,
  content, computed indicator), sorted by title A–Z.
- Selecting a row selects that atom everywhere: the row reflects the shared `selectedAtomId`
  and opens the inspector, and selection carries over to Flow/Network.
- Editing an editable cell (title, content, labels) persists via the shared atom-update
  mutation and the change is reflected in the graph views without a manual refresh.
- Atoms with a computation (`operation != null`) render their content read-only.
- A viewer-level atom (effective access level VIEWER) exposes no editable cells (respects
  ADR-260059).
- Category-facet and compute-status columns are out of scope here and are added by their
  owning epics (EPIC-260068 / EPIC-260069) without reworking the view.
